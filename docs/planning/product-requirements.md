# Product Scope and MVP Decision Input

**Purpose:** This is the concise decision record for CINE-001, not a second requirements specification. The canonical detailed requirements and acceptance journeys are in [requirements.md](requirements.md); the product vision is recorded in the repository [README](../../README.md) and requirements document.

**Status:** MVP scope and success measures are not approved. The eventual product capabilities are committed by the kickoff; CINE-001 decides MVP sequencing and outcomes, not whether those capabilities remain in the product vision.

## Product boundary

CINEFLOW is a movie discovery and streaming platform. Its eventual capabilities include accounts and profiles; movie catalog, genre/category browsing, search and filtering; authorized adaptive playback and subtitles; watchlists, favorites, continue-watching, progress and history; admin/content operations and rights verification; recommendations; and analytics. Every specific title is available only where documented rights permit the relevant use, territory and time.

The MVP boundary, launch markets, user cohorts, success measures, sequencing and detailed behavior remain to be approved. Exact source/provider mechanics, whether media is first-party hosted/transcoded or delivered by an authorized third party, legal/privacy policy, analytics collection and retention, and operating constraints remain open. Processing implementation may depend on the selected delivery model; this does not make authorized playback optional in the eventual product.
